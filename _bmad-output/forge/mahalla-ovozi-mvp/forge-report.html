A private district monitoring product that turns informal Telegram group conversation into cautious, same-day Topics with original evidence for direct hokim review.

3–4 districts 120 groups Private paid software Uzbekistan calendar-day Topics

Outcome

The idea is specific enough to enter formal product planning. Its central AI/Topic mechanism, business model, direct-user workflow, access model, subscriptions, operating scale, and MVP boundaries are decided. Fourteen adversarial findings were resolved or deliberately routed to technical research and architecture.

Locked product spine

Direct user and customer

Hokims privately commission district-scoped deployments and monitor their own dashboards directly. No staff reporting layer sits between the product and the hokim.

Private multi-customer MVP

Support 3–4 isolated districts. The Product Owner manages customers and manually administered subscriptions. This is not public SaaS.

Semantic triage

Human text and captions are interpreted by meaning, intent, context, slang, mixed scripts, and typos. Vocabulary helps the LLM but never forces classification.

Daily Topics

One underlying situation per district, mahalla, and Uzbekistan calendar day. Raw accepted evidence drives cautious summaries and dynamic lane membership.

Hokim-related complaints

An independent complaint category may include issues outside Water, Electricity, Gas, and Waste. Overlapping signals remain one canonical Topic shown in multiple lanes.

Evidence over claims

Captured messages remain primary evidence. The product attributes resident reports, preserves disagreement, and does not claim objective truth or resolution.

Future-only AI changes

Model, prompt, and vocabulary changes activate immediately for future processing. Old decisions are never replayed; every result records its configuration version.

Operations before immediacy

Durable ordered processing and no message loss matter more than real-time display. System Health exposes connection, queue, AI, and failure state to the Product Owner.

Focused Hokim interface

One compact sticky toolbar replaces sidebar and page navigation. A Today-default unified dashboard filters and searches retained 90-day history across five independently scrollable lanes; evidence opens in an overlay drawer.

Evidence-based statistics

A compact filter-aware strip shows deterministic Topic, Hokim-related, active-mahalla, leading-service, and leading-mahalla measures. Trends are neutral; AI mood analysis is outside MVP.

Key operating limits

Capacity envelope

  • Up to 4 districts and 120 groups.
  • 20,000 messages per day.
  • Short bursts of 100 messages per minute.
  • 10 simultaneous dashboard sessions.
  • Approximately 180,000 retained evidence messages.

Retention and search

  • Topic-level evidence retention for 90 days.
  • Irrelevant production messages discarded immediately.
  • Today-default unified dashboard filtering and plain-text search across retained 90-day evidence.
  • Large result sets load progressively inside independently scrollable lanes.

Subscription lifecycle

  • Active → seven-day Grace → Suspended → Cancelled.
  • Suspension stops new ingestion and hokim access.
  • Reactivation resumes future messages only.
  • Cancellation provides 30 recovery days, then permanent deletion.

Roles and access

  • Only Product Owner and Hokim application roles.
  • Hokim sees one district.
  • Product Owner has disclosed standing access across customers.
  • District-owned isolated Telegram bots.

Statistical interpretation

  • Activity means unique Topic count, not message volume.
  • Multi-category Topics count once in each applicable service.
  • Evidence count is secondary context.
  • Filter-aware period comparisons carry no automatic good/bad judgment.

Rejected directions

Complex historical behavior

Rejected rolling 24-hour Topics, cross-day continuation, retroactive recovery, and production historical replay.

Brittle classification

Rejected keyword admission/rejection, planned-announcement Topics, ambiguous-message pools, and summary-only or vector context.

Workflow expansion

Rejected case management, staff review chains, ticketing, assignments, SLAs, resolution tracking, and manual Topic correction.

Public SaaS machinery

Rejected public signup, advertising, marketing funnels, public checkout, automatic charging, and unnecessary application roles.

Weak points that survive scrutiny

  • The local AI stack has not yet proved the full 20,000-message design envelope with all accepted same-day evidence context.
  • The 5/15-minute latency numbers are targets awaiting technical validation, not commercial guarantees.
  • Missed signals cannot be recovered from production because irrelevant text is deliberately not retained.
  • Customer adoption still depends on hokims voluntarily using the dashboard; the product avoids manufacturing an institutional workflow to force usage.
  • Telegram source navigation can fail even when captured dashboard evidence remains intact.
