## 2. What Went Well

1. **Deterministic Health Hierarchy & Quiet Handling:**
   * The health evaluation state machine (`Unavailable > Degraded > Delayed > Unknown > Healthy`) with neutral `Quiet` handling successfully prevents false alarm fatigue when Telegram channels are legitimately inactive.
2. **Strict Privacy Perimeter in Telemetry:**
   * Health states, diagnostic logs, and troubleshooting views completely isolate resident messages and credentials from operational monitoring.
3. **Idempotent Queue Replay:**
   * Retry mechanisms within the pg-boss job processor are guarded against duplicate signal creation, ensuring safe recovery from transient failures.
4. **Architecture Deepening & Consolidation:**
   * Completed a proactive 5-layer refactoring across domain, application, infrastructure, contracts, and UI to ensure consistent conventions ahead of configuration versioning.

---

## 3. Key Learnings & Takeaways

* **Lesson 1 (Truthful Status):** Stale or insufficient telemetry must yield `Unknown` rather than optimistically assuming `Healthy`.
* **Lesson 2 (Audit Coupling):** Operational changes must generate structured, tamper-evident audit logs at the point of mutation, not via decoupled side effects.
* **Lesson 3 (Contract-First Isolation):** Strict API contract validation prevents raw database errors and third-party SDK responses from leaking to client interfaces.
